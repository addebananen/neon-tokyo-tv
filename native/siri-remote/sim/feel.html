Siri Remote feel test

Swipe on the clickpad as you would on an Apple TV. Slow slides step one tile at a time, quick flicks glide and slow down, a touch stops the glide. Tap the centre to click, tap the ring's edges for arrows. This runs the exact Rust engine that goes on the TV.

Waiting for your finger…

Hold TV for quick settings, press it twice for recent apps. Hold Back to go Home.

Scrolling

About a fifth of the pad per tile when you slide slowly. The faster the finger, the less travel each tile takes, up to three times less.

Glide

Lift during a quick swipe and focus keeps moving, slowing down over about a third of a second. Touch the pad and it stops where it is.

Circle to scrub

Run a finger round the outer ring: clockwise steps forward, anti-clockwise back, like seeking in a film on the Apple TV.

On the TV

The same engine runs in Neon Tokyo's Siri Remote bridge and presses these keys in every app, on Neon Home and over the consoles.
